Pagination

Everything below exists to give the page-accurate layout something to paginate. Turn on the layout view and the document flows into real pages at the size and margins from Page setup, with the status bar showing which page the cursor is on.

Scrolling stays in step with the live preview: the syntax markers for whichever line holds the cursor reveal themselves as it arrives and hide again as it leaves, so text never shifts under you while you read.
